About This Project

Mercedes-Benz set out to launch the G-Class G 63 and Electric G-Class across the GCC with a campaign built specifically for social media.

Working in collaboration with Team X, AstroShot developed and produced a regional mixed reality campaign spanning Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Kuwait, Riyadh and Qatar.

The Challenge

Launching one of Mercedes-Benz's most iconic luxury vehicles required content that maintained the premium character of the G-Class while creating the kind of unexpected, entertaining moment that earns attention organically on social media.

The scale added another challenge: five cities, five concepts, five influencers and five separate productions, all forming part of one cohesive regional campaign.

The Creative Approach

AstroShot developed a different mixed reality and CGI concept around a recognizable setting in each city.

After developing the concepts and storyboards with Team X, our production teams captured the required footage across the five GCC locations and combined it with CGI and VFX to create approximately 12-second social-first films designed to blur the line between reality and digital content.

Five Cities. Five Experiences.

Dubai: Boxpark
Abu Dhabi: Emirates Palace Mandarin Oriental
Kuwait: The Avenues
Riyadh: Maraya
Qatar: Lusail Boulevard

Each film featured a local influencer, giving every market its own identity while keeping the overall G-Class launch visually connected.

AstroShot also created a dedicated teaser to build anticipation before the campaign rollout.

Results

5 GCC cities

5 mixed reality / CGI films
1 campaign teaser

The campaign delivered a coordinated GCC launch that combined luxury automotive storytelling with social-first mixed reality production, creating locally relevant content under one regional Mercedes-Benz campaign.
