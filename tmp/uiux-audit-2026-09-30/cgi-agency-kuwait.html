Frequently Asked Questions

What does a CGI agency do?

A CGI agency creates computer-generated visuals for advertising, product launches, commercial storytelling, and branded content. This includes 3D animation, product visualization, virtual scenes, and digital campaign assets.

How is CGI used in advertising?

CGI is used to create launch films, product reveals, mixed reality scenes, social ads, digital OOH, and campaign assets that would be difficult, expensive, or impossible to capture with live production alone.

Is CGI better than traditional production?

Not always — but for many campaigns, CGI offers more flexibility, more visual control, and stronger creative possibilities. It is especially effective for product storytelling, automotive launches, and digital-first campaigns.

How much does CGI production cost in Kuwait?

CGI production cost depends on concept complexity, number of shots, model/detail quality, animation, rendering, compositing, and deliverables. Astroshot scopes CGI around the campaign idea and asset list.

Can Astroshot create CGI for social media campaigns?

Yes. Astroshot creates CGI content for Instagram, TikTok, launch campaigns, digital ads, and broader social-first content systems.

Does Astroshot work with brands outside Kuwait?

Yes. Astroshot works with brands across Kuwait and the wider GCC, including Saudi Arabia and the UAE.
